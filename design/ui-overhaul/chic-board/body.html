4 “chic” directions · font + palette

Each card is one font and one palette, shown on the two surfaces that carry almost all of the game’s text: the field HUD over a real Port Sapphire frame, and the battle screen. Every direction is legible in Japanese and English — the sample glyphs are embedded, so this file renders the same with no network. Direction 0 is what ships today, for comparison.

Worth knowing before you pick: the font that ships today does not actually reach the phone. ui-overhaul.css pulls M PLUS Rounded 1c from Google Fonts over the network, and nothing is bundled into dist/fonts/, so in the offline Capacitor app every menu falls back to the system face. The in-game HUD never asked for it at all — it is hard-coded to the system stack. Whichever direction you pick, the font gets bundled locally and the three surfaces stop disagreeing.
