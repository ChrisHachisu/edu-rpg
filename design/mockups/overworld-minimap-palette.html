Overworld minimap — three palette directions

The HUD chrome moved to Charcoal & Gold Leaf. The minimap terrain did not: it still uses the original arcade palette, so saturated greens, primary blue and pure yellow sit inside a charcoal frame. Every map below is drawn by a faithful copy of the shipping drawFieldMap() over the real generated overworld, at the real on‑device size of 146 px inside the real 156 px gold frame. Only the colour table changes between variants.

The three directions

What the 21 tile indices actually are

Authoritative source: the legend at the top of src/utils/MapGenerator.ts, cross‑checked against OW_PROP and OW_BLOCK in public/dq-tiles.js. Counts are live from the generated 320×400 overworld after the mountain consolidation pass that dq-tiles.js applies to mapData in place.

Reading‑hierarchy gap, worth a separate look. drawFieldMap expands 6, 7, 9, 10, 12, 15, 16 to 2× and the castle (8) to 3×, but 11 (signpost), 19 (desert tomb) and 20 (desert signpost) draw at 1× even though OW_PROP treats all three as landmarks with their own art. At 1.83 px per tile the only desert tomb in the world is a two‑pixel speck. No variant here changes the sizes, so the comparison stays honest, but the fix is one line in the special test.

Cost, measured above rather than guessed. Every one of these maps redraws all 6,402 tiles as individual fillRect calls, exactly as the shipping code does, throttled to one draw per 220 ms. That is about 29,000 canvas operations per second of walking, all of them landing on the same 146 px square whose content only changes when the hero crosses a tile boundary.

Read it as a periodic main‑thread stall, not a constant drag: one blocking chunk of work roughly four and a half times a second, on the same thread as movement. On a desktop that is a couple of milliseconds; on a phone it is the length of a dropped frame or two, felt as a regular stutter while walking rather than as general slowness. The palette is not what costs this, so a palette change neither helps nor hurts it, but it is the natural moment to fix it: build the 80×80 map once into an offscreen canvas, rebuild only when the hero changes tile, and blit it. Recommendation only, deliberately not part of this change.
