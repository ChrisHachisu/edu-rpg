Battle commands, per-button tap response

Rebuilt against the shipped build, not against the tracked source. Every colour, size and radius here was measured off the device capture 06-battle-mid.png: one dark rounded bar, Attack / Defend / Item / Flee side by side, tinted glyph over a grey label, and the selected command carrying a solid gold fill with a 3px ring. The change: nothing is filled at rest, each button answers its own tap, and the gold fill comes back the moment you use the arrow keys.

  • Layout one horizontal bar, four columns, not a 2x2 grid
  • Order Attack, Defend, Item, Flee, left to right
  • Glyphs red sword #a8564e, blue shield, green flask, grey boot
  • Labels grey #a49e91, no per-command background
  • Selected solid #c9a961 fill, dark ink label, 3px #544934 ring
  • Around it enemy card with red HP above, hero strip with green HP below
  • Type the game's own Zen Maru Gothic, subset and embedded
  • Not matched the pixel-art background, flat gradient stand-in

Keyboard / gamepad simulator

Keys Input mode touch  ·  cursor Attack

Real arrow keys work too. Arrows put the gold cursor back on screen; the next tap or click hides it again. That is the whole rule: last input device wins, so keyboard and gamepad navigation is untouched and touch never shows a command as pre-chosen.

Where the numbers come from

Timing

  • input.feedback, under 80ms first response, from design/GAME-FEEL.md.
  • Press-in is not animated: the class lands on pointerdown and paints on the next frame, roughly 16ms. Well inside the token and inside the 100ms rubric.
  • The eased leg is the release: 70ms (A, C) or 90ms (B), ease-out. Both sit in the 50–100ms band.

Juice budget

  • Tier 1: one effect per tap. A changes background only, B changes transform only, C changes box-shadow only. Never two.
  • No particles, no shake, no hit-stop on a menu press.
  • No linear easing anywhere. Zero playful easings spent, so the one-per-screen allowance is left for the damage numbers.
  • Labels never drop below their idle contrast: the pressed gold is #a98a45, dark enough to keep the ink label legible for the whole 70ms.

Honest gaps

  • The battle background is a flat gradient, sampled from the capture. No art was copied into this page.
  • The arena is vertically compressed so four screens fit on one page; the card, bar and strip keep their real heights and insets.
  • Exact at 402px wide (iPhone 16 Pro). Narrower viewports shrink the bar proportionally, type stays fixed.
  • Sword, shield, flask and boot are redrawn from the capture, not the shipped asset; every glyph box lands within 2 device px of the real one, sub-pixel detail will not.
  • Resolved 2026-08-08. This page first shipped a warm tan sword, inferred because Attack carries the plate in every capture we hold and a plated glyph inverts to charcoal, so no capture had ever shown the unselected one. The owner corrected it and the codebase agreed: BATTLE_ACT already tints Attack --ruby #a8564e. It is red here now, and it is only visible at all because the resting selection is gone.
  • Selected sword stays charcoal. Red on the gold plate measures 2.27:1, under the 3:1 floor for a graphical object; charcoal on gold is 8.02:1. Unselected red on the opaque bar is 3.53:1. The plate now appears only for keyboard and gamepad players, so touch never sees anything but the red.
  • The wash costs contrast while it is held. Over rgba(201,169,97,.26) the glyphs drop to 2.13 / 3.19 / 3.56 / 4.08:1 and the labels to 4.08:1, for as long as the finger is down. Inherent to a background wash, not to this build; stated rather than quietly traded away.
