# Task: Port Sapphire — the wise woman's cottage, plus three missing town props

One square image, a 2 x 2 sheet of four separate objects, each centred in its own quadrant on a
FLAT MAGENTA background (255, 0, 255). No gutters, no labels, no border, no ground, no shadow cast
onto anything outside the object's own small contact shadow.

    TOP-LEFT      THE WISE WOMAN'S COTTAGE, version A
    TOP-RIGHT     THE WISE WOMAN'S COTTAGE, version B
    BOTTOM-LEFT   A WOODEN SIGNPOST
    BOTTOM-RIGHT  A COVERED MARKET CART

## STYLE — match the attached sheet exactly

The attached image is the approved prop set for this town. Match it: hard-edged pixel art, chunky
pixel blocks, dark outlines, stepped flat shading in two or three values per material, one
upper-left light source, a short soft contact shadow under each object. Slightly-above,
three-quarter view, all four objects seen from the SAME angle as the attached buildings.

Do NOT paint. No soft airbrushed gradients, no blur, no bloom, no photographic texture.

Saturation across the attached sheet measures 0.708 and reads slightly candy. Pull it back to
about 0.63 — the same colours, a little less sweet. Do not change the hues and do not darken:
mean luminance stays near 80.

## SCALE — the doors decide it

A door must be tall enough for a person to walk through. On the attached sheet the doors are
correct; match them. Each cottage is drawn at about the same size as the attached houses.

## THE FOUR OBJECTS

1. WISE WOMAN'S COTTAGE, VERSION A. A small fortune-teller's cottage. Deep blue-violet shingled
   roof, pale plaster walls with dark timber framing, one round window, a low stone step. Dried
   herbs and a string of small charms hang under the eaves. A short crooked chimney with a thin
   curl of smoke. Reads as old and lived in, a little mysterious, still cheerful daylight.

2. WISE WOMAN'S COTTAGE, VERSION B. Same character, different building: a round stone tower cottage
   with a conical blue-violet roof, a heavy arched door, a small lantern beside it, a stone bowl of
   water on the step, and a hanging sign carved with a crescent moon. Same palette as version A.

3. WOODEN SIGNPOST. A single weathered post with two carved arrow boards pointing opposite ways,
   iron nails, a little moss at the base. About one third the height of a cottage.

4. COVERED MARKET CART. A two-wheeled handcart with a striped canvas cover over hoops, crates and
   sacks in the bed, the shafts resting on the ground. Roughly as wide as a cottage door is tall,
   twice over.
