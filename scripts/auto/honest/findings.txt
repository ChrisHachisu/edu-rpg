# Honest player: difficulty report (track H)

Status: **DONE** for all five acts. Three child profiles, five full playthroughs each (48 story stops, 23 bosses, 20 tries per boss per run = 100 tries per boss per profile), plus two policy variants (two playthroughs each) and what-if runs on the two walls. Every fight ran in the game's real BattleScene with the hero record the game itself had built up to that point. Nothing in the game was changed.

The story as shipped cannot be finished by any of the three children: the last boss was never beaten (0 of 300 tries). A grade 6 child is also stopped at the very first boss in four runs of five. Between those two walls most of the game is too easy to matter: from the second dungeon to the end of Act 4 a random fight costs 0.1–3% of the hero's HP.

## 1. Ranked problems

Numbers are from the reference policy ("main": the brief's policy, plus selling outgrown gear) unless a variant is named. "L" is the mean hero level at the boss door. Re-run commands are in section 4.

**B2. A full bag (20 stacks) silently destroys boss rewards, quest rewards and chest items, and can close the story.**
`Player.addItem` returns false when the bag holds 20 different items; the boss reward code, `applyQuestRewards` and the chest code ignore that answer and still show the reward. Verified in the running game by `bagcap.cjs`: with 20 stacks, `addItem('flameCloak')` returns false, the game's own `applyQuestRewards` pays the gold and drops the item, the golden-chest Holy Amulet is refused, a legendary reward is equipped but the weapon it replaces is deleted, and taking a piece of armour off deletes it.
In the `nosell` variant (the brief's policy to the letter: she buys the best gear and never sells; 6 playthroughs) the bag is full after stop 17–25, the end of Act 2 or early Act 3: about ten stacks of potions, crystals and key items plus one stack per outgrown piece of gear. From then on, in 6 of 6 runs:
- the Flame Cloak (Sand Golem's reward) vanishes and Magma Tunnels, the only way into Act 4, stays closed ("no Flame Cloak in the bag"). The boss cannot be fought again, so the save is stuck for good;
- the golden chest's Holy Amulet vanishes (the chest is spent). Without it the Sword Wraith takes one tenth of the damage: 0/120 wins. No Excalibur means Demon Castle stays sealed;
- boss gear vanishes (Magma Blade, Molten Greaves and Bandit Dagger in 6/6, Sandstorm Cloak in 5/6) and the Ancient Relic quest's amulet in 5/6;
- every shop from Ruins Camp or Oasis Haven on answers "Inventory is full!" to every purchase, so she plays Acts 3 to 5 in the gear she wore when the bag filled (until the four legendary pieces equip themselves) and ends with 15,000–20,000 gold in her pocket (grade 6 then wins Malachar 2/40 and Storm Sentinel 1/40); chests drop 37 elixirs, 34 potions and 21 escape crystals on the floor over the 6 runs.
The runs were carried past the gate to measure the rest; see the `nosell` tables. Children hoard: this is the likeliest way a real save dies.

## 2. How it was measured

1. `world.cjs` reads the shipped bundle (`dist/assets/index-BhoGQRaA.js`: zones, monsters, items, shops, quests, maps) and the authored floor data, and builds the story line: 48 stops in order, for each dungeon floor the shortest walk door, route chests, stairs, crystal, boss (breadth-first over the floor grid), for each overworld leg the shortest walk over the game's own walkable raster. Steps = shortest route x 1.3 (wander).
2. `run.cjs` boots the game's own New Game record for the grade (captured through the real title screen: level 1, 40 HP, ATK 15, DEF 5, 3 herbs, 50 gold) and walks the stops. Each step calls the game's `EncounterManager.onStep` for the zone under her; each encounter is fought in the real BattleScene by `inpage.js`, which presses the scene's own menu, item and answer handlers. XP, gold, drops and level-ups are whatever the game awards; the record carries forward.
3. At each boss door the record is snapshotted and the boss is fought 20 times from it; she goes on with the first winning try (or is carried past a boss she never beat, which is listed as "forced").
4. Towns: quests through the game's QuestManager, healer at the town's price, best affordable weapon / armour / shield / helmet from that town's shop table, heal items up to a stock of 6 with at most half the remaining gold. Chests through the game's `getTreasureReward`; boss rewards as the game's wake handler gives them.
5. `report.cjs` aggregates the event logs into the tables below and `results.json`.

**Profiles (exact):**

| profile | quizDifficulty | right answers | answer time | monster scale | encounter rate | base timer |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| grade 1 | '1' | 70% | mean 6 s | x0.85 | x0.6 | 25 s |
| grade 3 | '3' | 80% | mean 4 s | x1.0 | x0.8 | 18 s |
| grade 6 | '6' | 90% | mean 3 s | x1.3 | x1.0 | 14 s |

Answer time is log-normal around the mean (sigma 0.35, floor 0.8 s). A wrong answer picks one of the wrong choices at random. Measured share of right answers in random fights over all runs: 70.8% / 79.5% / 89.8%.

**How the quiz setting and the timer work (checked in the bundle and in play):** the grade sets question content, the monster scale and encounter rate above, and the timer: base seconds by grade (K 30, 1: 25, 2: 22, 3: 18, 4: 16, 5: 16, 6: 14) x enemy tier (weak 1.5, normal 1, strong 0.85, mid-boss 0.9, boss 0.85, late boss 0.8, final boss 0.75), plus up to 3 s for a streak, never under 5 s. A right attack answer does x1.5 if given within 3 s, else x1.2 if at least half the timer is left, else x1; a wrong one does 25% (and, by `ui-overhaul.js`, cannot finish the monster). On defence a right answer with half the timer left cuts the hit to 60%, a slower right answer to 80%. Running out of time counts as wrong. With these profiles the 3-second bonus is what separates the grades: 2% of grade 1's attack turns get x1.5 (67% get x1.2), 20% of grade 3's (58% x1.2), 50% of grade 6's (39% x1.2).

**Policy (exact):** attack every turn; use a heal item when HP is under 35% (the smallest one that restores at least 70% of what is missing); flee only from a random fight, under 20% HP, with no heal item left; never flee a boss; never defend. Between fights she tops up with items under 35% HP. A crystal on floor 2 or deeper restores HP (the game's "Restore HP"). Route chests are opened when they cost at most 20 extra steps; the golden chest always. A death to a random fight applies the game's rule (back to the last town, half the gold, full HP) and she walks back in from floor 1; after 6 deaths in one place she is carried on and the place is listed as forced. A boss loss is a free retry from the door, as in the game.
Variants: `main` sells gear she has outgrown (the reference: without it the bag fills, see B2); `nosell` is the brief's policy to the letter; `savvy` also heals under 50% HP against a boss and defends on the turn after "is gathering power".

**Speed-up, and why it is safe:** no time scale is changed. Scene methods are called directly instead of waiting for tweens; the scene clock is stepped by hand over the game's one-second pause after an answer; the timer bar is set to where the game's linear tween would stand after her thinking time, and a thinking time longer than the timer calls the scene's own timeout. One fight takes 130–250 ms, a playthrough about 2.5 minutes. Check against real play (`validate.cjs`): the same fight driven by real screen taps at 4 s per answer gave the engine a timer ratio of 0.773–0.774 and 4.07–4.08 s elapsed; the fast driver gives 0.778 and 4.0, the same sequence of engine calls, and damage inside the bundle's formula. Encounter rolls: exactly one per cell walked (6/6, 6/6, 12/12 in `calib.cjs`).

## 3. Limits of the method

- The walk is a model: shortest route x 1.3. Dungeon puzzles, keys, traps, the maze hunter and lost time are not played; real children wander more, which means more fights, more XP and more gold than measured here. Act 1 overworld legs and two legs without a path in the raster use straight-line distance x 1.2.
- The child never defends in `main`. That is the brief's policy and it is what decides M1; the `savvy` columns show the other end.
- Bosses she could not beat are passed by force so that the rest of the story can be measured. Everything after stop 2 at grade 6 assumes she somehow got past Mosswarden.
- Her answers are drawn from a seeded generator; the game's own dice (`Math.random`: damage roll, encounters, drops, chests) are not seeded, so two runs with the same seed differ. Boss rates rest on 100 tries (about +/-10 points at 95% around 50%), variants on 40.
- Quests: only the ones on the story line and those with a giver in the shipped game are taken. Luna's Prophecy (Obsidian Cavern) is not taken; the cavern is measured as an optional side trip on a copy of the record.
- Sunken Cellar and other side dungeons are not on the route. Chests further than 20 steps off the route are left closed. Mimic chests are not played: every chest pays the game's treasure table.
- Gear is compared by ATK + DEF only.
- Time per fight is an estimate (thinking time + 2.8 s of the game's own pauses per answer + a few seconds per fight).
