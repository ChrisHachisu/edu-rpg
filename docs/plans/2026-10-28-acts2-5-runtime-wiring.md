# Acts 2–5 runtime wiring (overworld, towns, dungeons)

Date: 2026-10-28 · Branch `acts2-5-world-wip` (build 91, da24147) · Status: design + flag-gated prototype (OFF by default)

This plan covers how to ship the Acts 2–5 overworld, town and dungeon art using the same
mechanisms that ship Act 1 in build 91. Act 1 is the reference implementation. Section 1 lists
everything in it that only works for Act 1. Sections 2–8 describe the generalisation. Section 9
covers the prototype that is already on the branch and how it was verified.

---

## 2. Manifest: one index, per-act folders

```
public/acts-hifi/manifest.json            schema qok-acts-hifi-v1 (prototype on branch)
public/act{N}-hifi/manifest.json          per-act chunk manifest, same schema family as act1-hifi
public/act{N}-hifi/chunks/<layer>/cC-rR.webp
public/act{N}-hifi/collision.bin          QOKF8COL bitset, act-local origin
public/act{N}-hifi/landmarks/…            sprites + landmarks.json
```
* The index lists, for each act: `bounds`, `origin`, `cols`/`rows`, `drawRank` (composite
  order 1,2,3,5,4 from `scripts/acts/acts_common.py`), `chunkPath`, `layers` and `collision`.
  `chunkPath:null` means use placeholders.
* It also carries every remapped door (section 4).
* Act 1 stays on `act1-hifi/` unchanged. Its gate pins and 30+ regression tests stay valid.
* Generate the index with `scripts/build_acts_hifi_manifest.py` (on the branch).

## 3. Streaming outside Act 1

Plan: refactor `A1A` into a per-act record (`ACTS[n] = {manifest, S, chunks, lru, forest, underpaint}`).
* `a1aChunkAt(tx,ty)` finds its act from a 320×400 act-ownership raster (128 KB, from
  `acts_common.world()`), then indexes `(tx-ox)>>4, (ty-oy)>>4` directly instead of scanning
  120 chunks.
* **One global LRU across acts**, so crossing a separator never holds two acts' windows.
* Prefetch margin is in **cells**, not chunk rings. Measured in the prototype on the phone
  viewport: a one-chunk ring kept 20 chunks resident, a 4-cell margin kept 9.
* **Overlaps.** Acts 1/2 share x 161–163. Acts 3/4 share y 88–128. Draw by `drawRank` and clip
  each chunk with the ownership raster (bake the clip into the art, as the overworld agent's
  composite already does). Act 1 keeps depth 1.1; other acts sit at 1.05 + rank·0.001 until they
  have real art.
* Water schema: allow `act{N}-water-*` schemas, or one shared material per biome (frozen water,
  oasis, lava).

## 4. Doors, landmark relocation, compass (prototype implements this)

This generalises the `act1-world-map.js` wrappers, driven by the index:
* `ENTRY[ownerCell]` returns the bundle's own `{targetMap,toX,toY,toFloor}`.
* A base result whose `(targetMap,toX,toY,toFloor)` belongs to a remapped door is nulled. This
  also kills the hard-coded bandit door.
* An interior's return to its **old** overworld cell is rewritten to the owner exit cell. The key
  is the old return cell, so two-mouth dungeons work.
* `getCompassTarget` rewrites frozen `ox/oy` to the owner cell. This covers `COMPASS_CHAIN`
  (bundle `hs` array) and `getOverworldCoordsForTarget`, which uses `fromX/fromY`. One alias:
  the chain uses 278,95 for scorchedRuins.
* **The bundle is not edited**, so `COMPASS_CHAIN` stays frozen and is corrected at read time,
  exactly as Act 1 does.

Door mapping: 33 owner doors map to bundle destinations. Only the overworld side moves, which is
the Act 1 rule.
* Separator mouths use `toFloor`: Crystal A2 floor 5, Shadow A3 floor 5, Magma A4 floor 5,
  Volcanic A5 floor 7, and the Haunted Forest 2nd entrance floor 5.
* Unmapped: **Cinderwatch** (257,42) has no map in the bundle. Leave it as open ground with no
  door (the Darkfang precedent).

**Collision (not in the prototype).** Generalise `act1_owner_paint_plate.mjs` to every act:
* `TILE_BY_PAINT = {'.':0,F:3,M:4,W:2,R:1}`, sea is 2, and doors are tile 6/7/15.
* Write the result to `public/acts-world-map.js`, with per-act `ROWS`, `SAFE` cells and
  relocation, and a `canMove` that blocks 3 everywhere.
* `a1fHeroFree` becomes per-act: subtract the act origin and use the act's bitset dims from its
  manifest.
* Until an act ships, its plate is not written, so the old procedural geography stays and is
  playable.

## 7. Memory and download budget (iPhone)

Measured, Act 1:
* `act1-hifi` is 145 MB: overworld chunks 111 MB, town 26 MB.
* By layer: water-r2 is 54 MB of **PNG** (median 556 KB per chunk), forest 26 MB PNG,
  base 17 MB WebP, crest 11 MB PNG.
* Decoded at 512 px, a chunk is about 5.8 MB of CPU bitmaps plus about the same on the GPU. That
  is roughly 11–12 MB per chunk, so the 12-chunk cap is about 140 MB. The 49.3 MB-per-chunk
  figure in the dq-tiles comments dates from the 1536 px bake.
* `act1-dungeon-art` is 136 MB: 12 RGB PNG floors at about 8.5 MB each, 3168×2544, about 32 MB
  decoded per floor.

Re-encoding to WebP (measured on 10-chunk samples; needs visual QA, especially the premultiplied
crest):

| layer | q90 size vs now | q80 |
|---|---|---|
| water-r2 PNG | **6 %** | 4 % |
| forest PNG | 21 % | 15 % |
| crest PNG | 53 % | 45 % |
| base WebP | 87 % | 61 % |

Proposed budget:
* **Overworld art, per act: at most 40 MB.** WebP q85 for every layer, alpha lossless. Skip
  sea-only and unowned chunks (Acts 2–5 have 55 of 430). Use the shared water material as a
  TileSprite (as Act 1's R2 already does) instead of a per-chunk water layer on all-land chunks.
  Act 1 alone would drop from 111 MB to about 35 MB. Acts 2–5 have 430 chunks, 3.6× Act 1, so
  about 110 MB for all four.
* **Collision:** about 3.5 MB raw per act. Ship it deflated, or as an 8 px-per-cell pack (1/16
  the size) for acts without whole-object forest.
* **Runtime:** one global cap of 12 chunks (about 140 MB), a 4-cell margin, and the battle drop
  generalised to all acts. Off the overworld, keep only the departure window, as Act 1 does.
* **Dungeons and towns are the real risk.** Act 1's style at about 34 MB per dungeon, times
  about 20 Acts 2–5 dungeons, is about 700 MB. Re-encode to WebP at about 10–15 % size and cap
  floor art at 2048 px per side, or tile it.
* **Lazy per-act download** (optional): bundle Acts 1–2 in the app and fetch Acts 3–5 packs
  (Capacitor Filesystem cache) on reaching the Shadow pass. This needs hosting and offline
  handling. Not needed if the budget above holds (an app under about 400 MB total).

## 11. Real Act 2 export in the game (2026-10-28, scratch dist only)

* **How the export was wired.** `acts-runtime.js` streams an act whose index entry sets
  `runtime: 'act2-hifi/'`. The export format is `flattened-ground-v0` (43 MB on disk).
  * Layers: ground at depth 1.09, crest0–2 at 1.091 cross-faded, front at 10.5.
  * The act's landmark sprites draw at depth 9.6 or 10.5, depending on the hero's row.
  * `plate.json` comes from `scripts/build_act_plate.py`: a cell counts as blocked when more than
    half of its `collision.png` pixels are blocked. It is written into mapData the
    act1-world-map way.
  * The legacy dq props inside the act (including props in its prop container) are hidden every
    frame, on the `prerender` event.
* **Screenshots** are in `/mnt/project-files/edu-rpg/acts-preview/ingame/`: snow near Ironkeep,
  two mountain/forest-meets-sea shots, the Act 1/2 seam in both draw orders (they come out almost
  identical), and the Ironkeep town plate.
* **Metrics** (Chromium with SwiftShader, so CPU-bound at about 4 fps; compare the two only
  relative to each other):

  | | mean frame time | textures | texture size | JS heap |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Act 2 | 233–239 ms | 803 | 85.6 MB | 180–190 MB |
  | Act 1 | 210–281 ms | 354 | 105–109 MB | 125–133 MB |

* **Flag OFF:** Act 1 is still pixel-identical to the build 91 shell (Greenhollow and Port
  Sapphire, 2 runs per arm, 0 differing pixels).
* **Parts of the export that do not fit the runtime:**
  1. `collision.png` is not a QOKF8COL `.bin`. Decoding it in the browser would cost about 110 MB
     of RGBA. With only the cell-level plate, the hero can overlap rock and tree pixels, so
     dq-tiles needs a per-act `a1fHeroFree` reading a `.bin`.
  2. The town json `exit.toX/toY` uses maps.ts (200,321). `handleTownExit` bypasses
     `checkTransition`, so the town json must carry the owner exit (209,321). This was patched in
     scratch only.
  3. The `town/npc/ironkeep-*.png` sheets are missing. Without them the town shows "could not
     finish loading". Act 1 sheets were substituted in scratch.
  4. The README contradicts itself on seam draw order: the Act 2 section says Act 2 goes on top,
     and "Known gaps" says Act 1 goes last.
  5. The legacy dq prop textures are still generated for the plate's tiles (803 textures
     against 354). The proper fix is for dq-tiles to skip prop generation inside a real-art act,
     the way `a1aPlateCell` does for Act 1.
  6. The `TOWN_IDS` patch was applied to the scratch dist only. `adapter.js` is a pinned file.
